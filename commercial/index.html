Restaurant Services

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater shows up first as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and a backed-up dish room. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which usually means a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and sizing for peak demand, with service scheduled around your hours.

A dish room without enough hot water can't sanitize, and health inspectors notice lukewarm handwashing sinks right away. Our service keeps temperatures and recovery where your kitchen needs them, backed by written reports and service logs after each visit. That paperwork shows a well-maintained kitchen to inspectors and landlords.

We work around your business, not the other way around. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and downtime stays short so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect venting and draft, confirm adequate combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, verify expansion tanks are charged to the right pressure, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. We pull permits and schedule inspections where required. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Regular professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

After-Hours Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

When a commercial water heater fails, the clock starts immediately. Dish rooms stall, salon chairs empty, guests call the front desk, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast for emergency repairs anywhere in Poinciana and the surrounding area, and planned work can be done overnight or before opening to limit downtime.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. Our first priority is diagnosing the fault and getting hot water back as quickly as possible, often by isolating the failed heater so the others carry the load. After that, we set up a maintenance schedule to prevent a repeat, and each response is fully documented for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Stopping failures before they happen is the most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters. Every Poinciana Water Heater program is tailored to the property's equipment, usage, and budget, and includes scheduled inspections, sediment flushing, anode rod checks, descaling for tankless units, relief valve testing, burner and vent inspection, recirculation and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the fixtures.
